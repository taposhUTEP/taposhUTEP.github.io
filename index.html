    About Me

    I earned my Ph.D. in Computer Science from The University of Texas at El Paso in 2026. Prior to my doctoral studies, I built a decade-long career as a software engineer, developing software for both the corporate and government sectors, including defense.

    My research centers on Cyber-Physical Systems (CPS), Time-Sensitive Networking (TSN), Artificial Intelligence in resource-constrained environments and edge computing. Professionally, my technical expertise spans software requirements specification (SRS), API and database design, data engineering, and full-stack web development.
